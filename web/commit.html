Commit, then discover

Sign the list once. Every later lookup starts from it.

A sweep reads what your wallet actually holds across the chains you pick. You review the exact chain-and-contract list, sign it — no gas, nothing is sent — and the daemon keeps it under your address. The Overview page applies it as the filter: your lookup reads those contracts first instead of spending calls rediscovering them. The list is public by design: you signed an enumerable disclosure.
