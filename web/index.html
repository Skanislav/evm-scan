evm-scan

Permissionless asset indexing over your own node. Contracts are found by watching the head, or registered on-chain as hints. Only promoted ones get a full history walk — down to whatever history the node kept, never assuming genesis. The result is a per-account discovery index, committed back on-chain as a merkle root.
